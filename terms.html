6. Prices, ratings, indices and store data

  • We do not verify ratings. We do not check whether a rating, price or ripeness score comes from a person who actually bought or tasted the banana. Our automated check only confirms that a photo shows a banana. "Also tasted" is an unverified statement by another user.
  • User observations. Each individual entry reflects what one user saw or tasted at one moment in one shop. It is that user's statement, not ours, and not an offer, price list or statement by the retailer or brand concerned.
  • Mistakes happen. A user can mistype a price (1.59 instead of 1.79), pick the wrong store or misjudge ripeness. Do not rely on any single entry for a purchase or business decision. If you see an entry that is wrong, report it in the app; the author can edit it, and we may correct or remove it.

  • Rankings and indices we calculate. We calculate them automatically from user entries. Common rules for all of them: entries by Bananiv people are left out (section 1); a result is shown only when it is based on at least 5 entries from at least 3 different users; the number of entries is shown with it.

    • Ripeness index of a retail chain: posts and "Also tasted" confirmations from the last 90 days, grouped by chain and country, simple average of ripeness scores (1–5), plus the share of green (1–2) and overripe (5) bananas.
    • Brand and retailer rankings (statistics): ordered by a weighted taste score that pulls small samples towards the overall average — (number of posts × average taste + 3 × overall average) ÷ (number of posts + 3) — so that a few extreme ratings cannot put a brand or chain at the top. The Bananiv brand is not ranked.

    These figures summarise a small number of unverified individual observations. They are not an audit, test or assessment of any retailer, brand or product. - Map data. Store locations come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors) and may be incomplete or outdated. Apart from what is said in section 1, we have no business relationship with the retailers or brands shown unless we say so.
